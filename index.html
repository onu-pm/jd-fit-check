    Your report shows up here

    1. ATS screen score: how likely the resume clears an automated keyword screen for this JD, from keyword coverage, readability and hard requirements.
    2. Fit score: how well you match the role, split into skills and domain, responsibilities, seniority and title.
    3. Matching skills: what you already have, shown next to the JD's wording for it.
    4. What to add or tailor: a checklist in priority order, with where each change goes.
    5. Bullet and summary rewrites in Google X-Y-Z format, gaps, and how to handle each one.

    The readability check on the left runs as soon as you upload. Your resume is kept only in this browser and sent to the AI model just for the check.

    Both scores are this page's own estimates. No ATS publishes a universal pass score: some only filter on knockout questions and keywords, and many leave ranking to a recruiter. Use the scores to find gaps, not as a target. Your resume and notes stay in this browser and are sent to the AI model only when you press Check fit.
